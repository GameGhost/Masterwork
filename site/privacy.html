Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: Masterwork collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. Your saves and settings stay on your device.

This policy covers every edition of the Masterwork app (including My Father's Work) on the web, Windows and Android, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't ask for your name, email or any account, and doesn't send usage data or identifiers to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, saved games, the player and town names you enter, and the scenarios you install.
  • On Windows and Android, a diagnostic log file the app writes to help fix crashes.
  • In the browser, the same information kept in your browser's local storage for this site. The app doesn't use cookies.

None of this leaves your device unless you choose to share it, for example by exporting a save or attaching a log file to a bug report. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, deletes it.

On Android, if you have device backup turned on, Android may include your saves and settings in the backup it keeps in your own Google account. We never receive it.

What the app downloads

To list and install scenarios, the app downloads a catalog, preview images and scenario files from its edition's website (for My Father's Work, myfatherswork.masterworkapp.com), hosted on Microsoft Azure. As with any website, those requests reach the host with your IP address and basic browser or device details. The hosting provider may keep standard operational logs; we don't use them to identify anyone.

If you add a scenario source yourself, the app downloads from that address instead, and its operator's own privacy practices apply.

App stores and downloads

If you install Masterwork from Google Play or the Microsoft Store, that store handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy. Stores give developers only aggregate statistics, such as install counts. Downloads from GitHub Releases are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

This site is a set of static pages with no cookies, analytics or tracking. Its hosting provider may keep standard operational logs, as above.

Children

Because Masterwork collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Masterwork ever starts collecting any data, this page will say exactly what and why before it happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@masterworkapp.com.
