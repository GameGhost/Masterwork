About Masterwork

An independent, open-source fan project.

Why it exists

Masterwork started as a way to keep My Father's Work playable and supported: community maintenance, translations, new features, and new scenarios for the game's companion app.

Underneath, it's built to be game-agnostic. An engine that runs the story, rewinds the timeline and keeps saves works for any branching narrative. New games can each release in their own themed edition of the same app, with their own sites and scenarios.

Open source

  • Masterwork: the app and engine, under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license.
  • Masterwork-Modules: the official scenarios, in an open format anyone can read and edit.

Where the content comes from

The My Father's Work scenarios, artwork, music and voice-over come from Renegade Game Studios' own community-resources release for the game, which invites the community to build on them. Each repository's notice files list exactly what was used.

Masterwork is an unofficial community project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Renegade Game Studios. My Father's Work and its names, trademarks, and artwork belong to their respective owners. For help with the board game itself, contact its publisher.
