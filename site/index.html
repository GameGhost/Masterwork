What you get

Play anywhere

The same app in your browser, on Windows, and on Android. Scenarios download inside the app, and your saves stay on your device.

Rewind the story

Step back through what's happened, change a choice and carry on from there.

Open to everyone

Scenarios are written in the Masterwork Module Format; an open format, so anyone can fix, translate or write them. The code is on GitHub.

Need a hand?

Found a bug, a typo in a scenario, or have an idea? See Help & feedback for where to report it.
