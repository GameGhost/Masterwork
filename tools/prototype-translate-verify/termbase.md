## Register

- Narration: literary first person, *passé simple* in past narration as the Cradle French does.
- Instructions to players: *vous*, imperative ("Gagnez 3 PV.", "Défaussez…"), as on rulebook cards.
- Buttons: infinitive ("Continuer", "Accepter"), as in the fr-CA app UI.

## Typography (fr-CA, normalized)

- No space before `?` `!` `;`.
- A non-breaking space (U+00A0) before `:`.
- Guillemets « » with a non-breaking space inside: « RÉVÉLER ».
- Amounts: "2 $" (space before $), "3 PV".
- Keep `**bold**`, `_italic_` and `//…//` exactly as the English string uses them, around the equivalent words.
